The firmware-equivalent replay of the revised embedded Pan-Tompkins-based detector was evaluated on all 48 MIT-BIH records (109,494 expert-annotated reference beats) and achieved gross sensitivity 0.9831, positive predictivity 0.9871, and F1 0.9851, with median matched-beat timing error 86.0 ms. The Hamilton comparator achieved sensitivity 0.9817, positive predictivity 0.9872, and F1 0.9844 under identical evaluation conditions. Controlled offline execution time of the firmware-equivalent implementation was 0.3146 s per approximately 30-min record (IQR 0.0052 s; 10.4547 ms/min ECG; real-time factor 0.000174); Hamilton required 0.2053 s per record (IQR 0.0398 s; 6.8220 ms/min ECG; real-time factor 0.000114). These values are controlled offline execution times and do not represent physical ESP32 latency. Record-level details are provided in Supplementary Figure Sx and Supplementary Table Sx.
