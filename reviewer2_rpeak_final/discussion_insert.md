In response to peer review, the optional embedded ECG-to-RR adapter was revised to use an established QRS-detection approach rather than the original prototype detector. This change strengthens the acquisition-specific route without altering the central acquisition-agnostic HRV architecture, which begins from normalized RR intervals and remains compatible with independent sources such as Polar H10. The MIT-BIH benchmark validates beat-detection agreement under offline replay conditions and should not be interpreted as clinical diagnostic validation of the complete AD8232/ESP32 hardware chain or of downstream HRV-derived physiological interpretation.
