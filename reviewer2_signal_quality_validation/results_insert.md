The corrected primary NSTDB analysis included 1296 non-overlapping 10 s windows composed of clean source ECG plus actual noise-exposed intervals. Using strict expert-annotation-derived RR integrity as the primary target, continuous Signal Confidence yielded AUROC 0.754 and AUPRC 0.545 for degraded RR windows, while the Orphanidou-style ECG SQI yielded AUROC 0.662. The existing non-Active Signal Status criterion had balanced accuracy 0.727; the Orphanidou binary SQI had balanced accuracy 0.632. Corrected actual-SNR summaries showed RR-intact window rates of clean: 99.4%, 24: 100.0%, 18: 94.9%, 12: 78.2%, 6: 14.7%, 0: 0.6%, -6: 0.0%. Signal Confidence declined most clearly when beat-sequence integrity degraded, supporting its interpretation as an RR-stream reliability indicator rather than a raw-ECG morphology SQI. Runtime QC showed median quality-assessment-only costs of 1.877 ms/10 s unit for Signal Confidence and 0.438 ms/10 s segment for Orphanidou SQI; complete-path costs were 3.647 ms/10 s segment for ECG-to-firmware-replay-to-RR-to-Signal Confidence and 1.576 ms/10 s segment for ECG-to-Hamilton-to-Orphanidou SQI.
